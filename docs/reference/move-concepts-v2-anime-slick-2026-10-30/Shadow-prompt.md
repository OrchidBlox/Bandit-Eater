# Shadow Moves prompt

Create one polished 16:9 Roblox PvP concept sheet in a dark concrete arena, with three rows (`1 SHADOW SHOT`, `2 SHADOW SNARE`, `3 UMBRAL BLINK`) and three equal panels per row labeled `WINDUP`, `RELEASE`, `IMPACT`. Use the same blocky yellow R15 caster in navy clothes and rival throughout each row. Match the anime-slick references: sharp tapered violet/black silhouettes, thin hard speed lines, hard two-tone cel shading, white-hot cores, impact stars, shards, streaks, sparks, mist, and inky ribbons. Palette: `#9B5CFF`, `#D9C2FF`, `#5A2DB5`.

Show exact bright floor telegraphs: Shadow Shot uses a thin 60-stud straight lane and releases a dark orb smeared into a violet arrowhead; Shadow Snare uses a radius-10 circle centered 19 studs ahead and raises binding ribbon tendrils that pull the rival inward; Umbral Blink uses a 14-stud-wide, 24-stud-long straight lane, with a shard-burst vanish, long slash afterimage, distant reappearance, and huge arrival crescent that launches the rival. Keep effects bounded by their hit areas, traveling in the caster's facing direction, large and immediately distinct. No projectile/dash circles, rounded cartoon forms, cloud-like white puffs, logos, watermark, extra panels, or misspelled text.
