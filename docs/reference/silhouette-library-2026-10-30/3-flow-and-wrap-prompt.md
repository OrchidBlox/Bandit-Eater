# Flow and Wrap Shapes Prompt

Create a landscape 16:9 concept sheet on an off-white background with an exact 4 by 4 grid. In each numbered cell, show one original anime-slick flow or wrap effect as a solid black silhouette on the left and the matching colored two-tone cel-shaded shape on the right. Use thin dark outlines, bright cores, hard planar facets, crisp edges, sharp tapered needle tips, and varied fire, wind, water, shadow, light, and earth palettes. Include, in order, these bold labels: TALL TORNADO, DOUBLE-HELIX RIBBON, S-CURVE RIBBON, FIGURE-EIGHT RIBBON, CURLING WAVE CREST, RISING FLAME SPIRAL, SERPENT COIL, TENTACLE CLUSTER, VORTEX DISC, CRESCENT WAKE TRAIL, FEATHER STREAM FAN, WHIP ARC, ORBITING BLADE RING, FIVE NEEDLE STREAKS, WING SWOOSH, DRAGON-HEAD FLAME. Make every silhouette distinct and readable. No characters, scenery, soft blobs, cloud puffs, logos, watermark, or extra text.
