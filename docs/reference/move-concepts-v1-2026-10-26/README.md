## Coverage

Six base sheets cover all 18 Q/E/R moves. Each shows windup, release and impact. Sword Q includes the small traveling slash required by the design. Sword E is explicitly a proposed parry interpretation of the legacy Blade Guard; the removed universal bubble shield is not included.

Two additional sheets cover all 18 slots in the six current prototype sword-fusion kits. These are illustrative, not approval of recipes or additional gameplay. Their Q/E slots reuse the corresponding base move identities; R is the prototype element Blade Burst.

Shared actions sheet covers M1 combo, dash, jump, double jump, inhale, timed catch, swallow, star spit and swap. No NPC bandit collection or bubble block.

| WIND | Q GUST: a compact fat curled air projectile moving forward, clearly catchable, not a beam. R CYCLONE: a tall plump spiraling tornado of 3 thick rounded bands, broad base warning ring before it rises; keep character visible. |
| EARTH | Q ROCK SHOT: a real chunky faceted rounded boulder propelled forward, NOT an energy ball; short white puff trail; catchable. R QUAKE: a committed ground stomp sends a broad thick ochre shock ring outward with chunky rounded rock chips hopping upward; warm readable warning ring before impact. |
| WATER | Q WATER SHOT: a fat teardrop water projectile with two small rounded trailing droplets, catchable, splats into round splash. E WHIRLPOOL: a shallow broad swirling water basin with thick spiral lip, inward motion and readable footprint. R TIDAL WAVE: one broad plump curling wave rolling forward, thick foam scallops and clear ground strip telegraph; miniature character clearly visible beside it. |
| FIRE | Q FIREBALL: a plump orange fireball with rounded three-lobed flame crown, short white puff trail and yellow star impact, catchable. E FLAME FIELD: a ground control ring containing several squat bouncy rounded flame blobs, preserve visibility through field. R ERUPTION: a ground warning ring followed by a single big rounded flame geyser with fat lobes, outward white cloud ring and sparse stars. No dark smoke or realistic fire. |
| SWORD | Q SLASH: one chunky sword swing launches a SMALL short-travel thick silver crescent, the traveling crescent is catchable but ordinary melee is not. | SHADOW | Q SHADOW SHOT: a friendly plump violet orb with a thick crescent-shaped inner cutout, white puff trail, catchable. R NIGHTFALL: a broad lavender warning disc followed by a large descending rounded violet crescent and a soft purple dome-shaped impact splash, short-lived with visibility around the target, never a full-screen blackout. |
