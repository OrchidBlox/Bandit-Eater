# Power Star — mesh review

Reference: docs/reference/move-concepts-v1-2026-10-26/power-emblems.png and power-swallow.png.
Build one reusable chunky, rounded five-point star, 2.8 studs wide, approximately 0.7 studs deep. Broad flat front leaves room for an interchangeable power emblem; no face, letters, or spikes. Gold preview material, softly beveled edges, under 1,500 triangles. Six base powers share the silhouette and use their emblem/palette. Fusion stars are not spawned.

Mesh stage only: preserve the live scene, author in a new scene, save separately, show front and three-quarter views. Bounce/spin are runtime transform animation and do not need a skeleton. Export follows owner silhouette approval.
